# The Science of Nutrition Timing

## What is Nutrition Timing?

Nutrition timing refers to *when* you consume nutrients relative to training, sleep, and daily activity patterns. While total caloric intake and macronutrient balance remain foundational, emerging research shows that the **timing** of nutrient delivery can significantly impact performance, recovery, and body composition outcomes.

## The Anabolic Window — Myth or Reality?

The "anabolic window" — the idea that you must consume protein within 30 minutes post-workout — has been both overstated and understated in fitness media. Here's what the science actually says:

### What We Know
- **Total daily protein** matters more than timing alone (~1.6-2.2g/kg bodyweight)
- **Pre-workout nutrition** (1-2 hours before) may be more impactful than post-workout
- **Fasted training** is effective for some goals but not all — context matters

### Practical Application
For most clients, I recommend:
1. A balanced meal 1-2 hours before training
2. Protein + carbs within 2 hours post-training
3. Consistent protein distribution across 3-4 meals daily

## Carbohydrate Periodization

Not every day requires the same carbohydrate intake. **Carb periodization** matches your carbohydrate consumption to your training load:

| Training Day | Carb Intake | Why |
|---|---|---|
| High-intensity / Heavy legs | High (5-7g/kg) | Glycogen replenishment, performance |
| Moderate / Upper body | Moderate (3-4g/kg) | Adequate fuel without excess |
| Rest / Light mobility | Low (2-3g/kg) | Let insulin sensitivity recover |

## The Bottom Line

Nutrition timing is a **performance optimization tool**, not a magic bullet. Build your foundation first — total calories, protein targets, and food quality — then layer in timing strategies for specific goals.
